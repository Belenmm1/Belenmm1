### 🎀 VECTOR Centro de Datos

**Operational Data Integration &amp; Traceability Platform** &nbsp; <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/status-in_development-D98AB8?style=flat-square" alt="In development" />

An independently initiated system project shaped by requirements discovery in a real business environment. Its goal is to bring operational information from different platforms into a consistent data layer, making day-to-day activity easier to trace, understand and manage.

**The information domains it brings together:**

| ♡ Domain | Operational information |
|:--|:--|
| 👥 **People &amp; attendance** | Staff records, work shifts and geolocated clock-in / clock-out data |
| 🧾 **Business operations** | Work orders, quotations, services, scheduling, payroll and financial movements |
| 🚚 **Fleet &amp; mobility** | Vehicle location and movement data |

The project combines systems analysis, workflow discovery, data modeling, interface development and integration work. A central challenge is turning information from separate sources — including sources with limited integration options — into consistent, auditable records.

### 🌸 PRISMA

**Vertical SaaS for Beauty &amp; Aesthetics Businesses** &nbsp; <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/real_product-8_local_clients-D98AB8?style=flat-square" alt="Real product with eight local clients" />

<p align="center"><em>“Less scattered information ♡ More operational control ♡ Better decisions.”</em></p>

PRISMA is a vertical SaaS product within the VECTOR ecosystem, designed for beauty salons, aesthetics centers and independent professionals. It brings everyday workflows that often live across WhatsApp, notebooks and spreadsheets into one organized business system.

| ♡ Module | What it helps manage |
|:--|:--|
| 📅 **Agenda** | Appointments and daily scheduling |
| ♡ **Clients** | Client records and service history |
| 🛍️ **Sales** | Sales and payment records |
| 💌 **Cash** | Daily cash control |
| 📊 **Reports** | Business metrics and operational insights |

PRISMA is being developed and improved with real businesses in Oberá, Misiones. Client conversations, onboarding and feedback shape the product's ongoing iterations.

### ☁️ DocuFlow

**Intelligent Document Processing SaaS**

An asynchronous document-processing application for PDFs, Word documents and spreadsheets. Its AI workflows support document classification, RAG-based document chat, FAISS semantic search, OCR fallback and AI-assisted comparison.

<details>
  <summary><strong>୨୧ Architecture &amp; technology</strong></summary>
  <br />
  A multi-service application with an API, background worker and Next.js frontend, including JWT authentication, Stripe payments, Alembic migrations, plan-based limits, Spanish / English localization and CI/CD on Railway.<br /><br />
  **Stack:** FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Redis · Celery · Docker · Next.js · OpenAI · Anthropic · Gemini · Ollama · FAISS
</details>
